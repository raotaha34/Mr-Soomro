Understanding PBN

What Are PBN Backlinks?

PBN stands for Private Blog Network. PBN backlinks are links placed on a network of websites that are used for SEO purposes.

A properly managed PBN campaign can give you greater control over link placement, anchor text, target URLs, and the overall structure of your backlink campaign.

Our Approach: We focus on relevance, domain quality, indexing, authority metrics, and natural-looking placements rather than simply delivering a large number of links.

Our approach focuses on selecting websites with useful SEO signals and placing links in relevant content.

Why Choose PBN Backlinks

  • Relevant link placements
  • Greater control over anchor text
  • Targeted links to specific URLs

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our PBN backlink service answered.

What is a PBN backlink?

A PBN backlink is a backlink placed on a website within a privately managed network of sites used for SEO purposes.

Are your PBN backlinks dofollow?

Yes, our standard PBN placements are generally provided as dofollow links, subject to the specific website and placement.

Are the PBN domains aged?

We prioritize aged or established domains where suitable domains are available for your campaign.

Can I choose the anchor text?

Yes. You can provide your preferred anchor text, brand terms, URLs, or keyword targets for each backlink.

Can you target specific pages?

Yes. You can provide specific URLs for your homepage, service pages, product pages, category pages, or other landing pages.

Can you target a specific niche?

Yes. We prioritize niche-relevant PBN domains whenever suitable domains are available in your industry.

Can you target specific countries?

Yes. Country-specific requirements can be included in custom campaigns, depending on available inventory.

Are PBN backlinks permanent?

We aim for long-term placements, but no third-party website placement can be guaranteed to remain online indefinitely.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No. We do not guarantee specific rankings. SEO performance depends on many factors including competition, content, technical SEO, website authority, search intent, and overall backlink profile.

How quickly will my backlinks be delivered?

Standard campaigns generally take around 5–14 days, depending on the package and available domains. Larger or highly specific campaigns may require additional time.

Can I order only 5 backlinks?

Yes. Our Starter package includes 5 PBN backlinks for $50, making it suitable for testing the service before moving to a larger campaign.
