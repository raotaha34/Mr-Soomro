FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our authority link-building campaigns.

How is this different from buying individual backlinks?

Individual backlink services provide a fixed number of links. A campaign takes a broader approach by combining research, strategy, target URL planning, anchor text planning, competitor analysis, and ongoing link acquisition into one structured process.

Do you analyze my competitors?

Yes. Competitor backlink research can be included to identify relevant referring domains and potential link opportunities that may benefit your campaign.

Can I choose the backlink type?

Yes. Depending on the campaign, you can request specific backlink types such as authority links, niche-relevant links, PBN links, or content-based placements.

Can you target specific countries?

Yes. Country targeting can be included depending on publisher availability. We'll work to find opportunities within your preferred geographic regions.

Can you work with competitive niches?

Yes. We can create campaigns for competitive and specialized niches, although requirements and pricing may vary depending on the niche complexity and publisher availability.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. We don't guarantee specific Google rankings. Search rankings depend on many factors, including competition, content quality, technical SEO, website authority, search intent, and the overall backlink profile.

Are backlinks permanent?

We target long-term placements, but third-party publishers control their websites, so lifetime retention cannot be guaranteed. We monitor placements and report any changes.

Do I receive reports?

Yes. Campaign reports include completed backlink placements and relevant campaign information. Reports are delivered monthly.

Can I cancel a monthly campaign?

Yes. Recurring campaigns can be reviewed and adjusted according to your requirements. Specific billing terms can be agreed before starting.

How quickly can the campaign start?

After receiving your website, keywords, target URLs, and requirements, we can begin the research and planning phase. Delivery timing depends on the campaign size and publisher availability.
