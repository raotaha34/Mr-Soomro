FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about our AI SEO and GEO optimization services.
What is GEO?
GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization . It focuses on optimizing content and websites for search experiences that generate AI-powered answers rather than showing a list of blue links.
Is GEO the same as SEO?
Not exactly. GEO builds on SEO principles but places additional emphasis on AI-readable content, entities, answer structures, topical authority, and generative search experiences.
Can you guarantee ChatGPT mentions my website?
No. AI-generated answers are controlled by external systems, and no legitimate provider can guarantee a specific citation or recommendation. We focus on making your site as AI-friendly as possible.
Can you optimize for Google AI Overviews?
Yes. We optimize content around relevant questions, search intent, topical authority, entities, and structured information that may support visibility in AI-powered search experiences.
Do you optimize for voice search?
Yes. We include conversational and question-based query optimization for voice assistants like Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant.
Do you provide content writing?
Content optimization and content creation can be included depending on the selected package. Larger packages include more comprehensive content work.
Do you optimize schema?
We audit and provide or implement appropriate structured-data recommendations depending on the package and website setup.
Can you optimize my existing content?
Yes. Existing pages can be analyzed and optimized for search intent, topical relevance, structure, entities, and AI-search readiness.
Will AI SEO replace traditional SEO?
No. Traditional SEO remains important. AI SEO and GEO should complement your overall organic search strategy, not replace it.
How long does AI SEO take?
AI SEO is an ongoing process. Initial optimization can be completed within 2–4 weeks , while measurable search and visibility changes depend on the website, competition, content quality, and search engine behavior.
