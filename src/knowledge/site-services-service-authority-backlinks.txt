Our Approach
What Makes Our Backlinks Different?
Quality Over Quantity
Fewer links, real value
We don't believe that hundreds of low-quality links automatically create better SEO results. Our approach focuses on acquiring fewer, more useful links from websites that can provide genuine SEO value.
Relevance First
Topical relevance matters
A relevant backlink can be more meaningful than a link from an unrelated website with a high authority metric. We therefore consider niche and topical relevance during site selection.
Multiple Quality Signals
Beyond DA or DR alone
We don't select websites based on DA or DR alone. We also consider organic traffic, indexing, content quality, topical relevance, website history, and overall backlink profile.
Natural Link Placement
Editorial, not forced
Our goal is to make backlinks look and function like genuine editorial references within useful content.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Are these backlinks permanent?
We target permanent placements, but no third-party publisher can guarantee lifetime availability. Publisher policies and website changes may affect link retention.
Are all backlinks dofollow?
We prioritize dofollow placements where available. The link attribute depends on the individual publisher.
Do you guarantee ranking improvements?
No. SEO rankings depend on many factors, including competition, website quality, technical SEO, content, search intent, and the existing backlink profile. We focus on building quality links that support your broader SEO strategy.
Can you target specific countries?
Yes. We can target publishers and websites based on your preferred country or audience where suitable websites are available.
Can you work with difficult niches?
We can review specialized and competitive niches and determine suitable publisher options before starting the campaign.
Can I provide my target keywords and URLs?
Yes. You can provide your target keywords, landing pages, preferred anchor text, and other campaign requirements.
Do you provide a backlink report?
Yes. A report is provided after the completed placements, including the relevant backlink information.
How long does the campaign take?
Typical delivery depends on publisher availability and the package selected. Most campaigns can take approximately 7–21 days, while larger or highly specific campaigns may require additional time.
