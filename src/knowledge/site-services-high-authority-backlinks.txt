FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about our high-authority backlink service.
What makes a backlink high authority?
A high-authority backlink generally comes from an established website with strong authority signals, quality content, relevant topical coverage, and a healthy online presence with genuine search visibility.
Do you use DA and DR?
Yes. DA and DR can be useful evaluation metrics, but we don't use them as the only criteria for selecting websites. We combine multiple signals for a comprehensive evaluation.
Do the websites have organic traffic?
We prioritize websites with established organic visibility where suitable opportunities are available. Traffic levels can vary by publisher and niche.
Are the backlinks dofollow?
We prioritize dofollow opportunities where available. The final link attribute depends on the publisher and placement policies.
Can I specify the minimum DA or DR?
Yes. You can provide your minimum authority requirements when ordering a custom campaign. Pricing may vary based on specific metric requirements.
Can you provide backlinks from specific countries?
Yes. Country targeting can be requested, subject to publisher availability in your requested geographic regions.
Can I choose my target URLs?
Yes. You can provide the exact URLs you want to promote — whether that's your homepage, service pages, product pages, or blog posts.
Can I provide my own anchor text?
Yes. You can provide preferred anchor text, although we recommend maintaining a natural and diversified anchor profile for long-term SEO health.
Are the backlinks permanent?
We target long-term placements, but third-party publishers ultimately control their websites and content, so lifetime retention cannot be guaranteed.
Do you guarantee rankings?
No. We don't guarantee specific Google rankings. Backlinks are one component of SEO, and results also depend on content, technical SEO, competition, search intent, and many other factors.
How long does delivery take?
Most campaigns take approximately 7–21 days , depending on publisher availability and campaign requirements. Larger or highly specific campaigns may take longer.
