How to Get Featured in Google AI Overviews — A Step-by-Step 2026 Guide
Jan 5, 2026
7 min read
AI Overviews are reshaping organic click-through rates in 2026. Google's AI-powered search results now appear
at the top of many queries, fundamentally changing how users interact with search. Here's how to optimise your
content to be featured.
What Are Google AI Overviews?
AI Overviews are AI-generated summaries that appear at the top of Google search results. They synthesize
information from multiple sources to provide direct answers, reducing the need for users to click through to
individual websites.
Why Being Featured Matters
While AI Overviews may reduce overall clicks, being cited as a source provides significant benefits:
Brand visibility - Your site is mentioned as a trusted source
Authority building - Google considers your content high-quality
Targeted traffic - Users who do click are highly qualified
Competitive advantage - Most businesses aren't optimizing for this yet
How to Optimize for AI Overviews
1. Create Comprehensive, Authoritative Content
AI Overviews pull from sources that thoroughly cover topics. Write in-depth content that answers related
questions comprehensively. Aim for 2,000+ words on important topics.
2. Use Clear, Direct Language
Write in a clear, straightforward manner. Avoid jargon unless necessary. Use short paragraphs and bullet points
to make information easily digestible.
3. Structure Content with Headers
Use H2 and H3 headers to break content into clear sections. This helps AI understand your content structure and
extract relevant information.
4. Answer Specific Questions
Include FAQ sections that directly answer common questions. Use question-based headers ("What is...", "How
to...", "Why does...")
5. Build Topical Authority
Cover topics comprehensively across multiple related articles. Google is more likely to cite sources that
demonstrate expertise across an entire subject area.
6. Include Data and Statistics
AI Overviews often cite specific data points. Include original research, statistics, and credible sources in
your content.
7. Optimize for E-E-A-T
Demonstrate Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness. Include author bios, credentials,
and cite authoritative sources.
The Future of Search
AI Overviews represent a fundamental shift in search. While they may reduce traditional organic traffic, they
also create new opportunities for visibility. The key is adapting your SEO strategy to focus on being a cited
source rather than just ranking in position one.
Start implementing these strategies now to stay ahead as AI continues to transform search in 2026 and beyond.
